# Data inventory and retention proposal

Draft, November 16, 2026. Static repository inspection; provider dashboards and browser storage have not been verified. See README for proposed periods and legal sources. Authorization is purpose-specific; a contract is not a blanket exemption from Colombian authorization rules.

| Fields / records | Collection and purpose | Required / sensitive | Basis and recipients | Retention and recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User email, name, auth ID, session and OTP records | Better Auth / login, authentication | Email needed; identifiers personal | Informed authorization for account use; security/legal exceptions only as applicable. Convex, Resend, Vercel | Account lifecycle: closure +60 days active deletion; OTP validity is 10 minutes, verify actual persisted cleanup; never log OTPs in production. |
| Organization name, slug, kind, city, address, WhatsApp, legal name, tax ID, logo | Onboarding/settings, operation and controller identity | Business identity/contact needed for lawful public notices; sole-trader data personal | Account and contract purposes; Convex, R2, Vercel | Active relationship + proposed closure window. Complete controller identity before enabling collection, without making all business fields public. |
| Member user ID, email, name, title, role, schedule, signature, active status; invitations and tokens | Team/settings, booking availability, access and attribution | Role needed; signature restricted; tokens secrets | Authorized professional operation; Convex, R2, Resend | Revoke invitations/sessions immediately when appropriate; preserve only record attribution/evidence that remains necessary. Never expose invitation tokens to analytics. |
| Client first/last name, phone, email | Staff intake and booking, identity/contact | Name/phone currently required; booking also requires email | Business basic-data authorization before persistence; Convex, Resend | Tenant schedule plus closure process. Evaluate making booking email optional if confirmations can operate without it; current email purpose is documented. |
| Document type/number, birth date, adult attestation | Client ficha, matching and age policy | IDs personal; exact DOB optional except justified need | Business informed authorization; Convex | Prefer adult attestation for basic booking; verify valid calendar dates and age when DOB supplied. Document why exact DOB/ID is needed for a procedure. No ID image by default. |
| Sex, occupation, home address | Ficha context | Optional personal fields | Specific relevant purpose required; Convex | Keep optional; do not collect by default without a documented use. No use for advertising. |
| Emergency contact name/phone | Ficha, emergency communication | Third-party data; optional | Business must establish notice/authorization or an applicable exception; patient consent is not automatically consent of the contact (verify) | Collect only if necessary; avoid expanded contact profiles; explain how the contact is informed. |
| Conditions, allergies, skin conditions, details, medications, pregnancy/lactation, skin type, Fitzpatrick, habits, previous treatment, health-review timestamps | Ficha and attention, safety assessment and follow-up | Health and related inferences sensitive | Prior explicit health authorization, distinct from booking; Convex | Tenant justified schedule (verify); prevent writes before authorization, including drafts and postponed visits. No blanket indefinite history. |
| Session assessment, contraindications, evolution, procedure fields/answers, aftercare, reasons, follow-up notes, case summaries | Sessions/cases, treatment record | Often sensitive, including custom fields/free text | Purpose-specific explicit authorization where required; Convex | Tenant schedule; no health in analytics, email subjects, logs, or public notes. Do not silently delete regulated records. |
| Client/professional signature keys, signed text, hash/version, time, device user agent, document text, historic guardian data | Authorization/signing and audit | Restricted evidence; signatures may involve sensitive use | Specific authorization/electronic-signature agreement; Convex, R2 | Separate minimal evidence from full health payload; five-year post-relationship proposal requires lawyer review. Preserve old snapshots without backdated acceptance. |
| Photo keys, kind, capture time, images, photo-use permissions | R2 upload/session compare | Identifiable/body/health content requires strong protection | Separate private-record photo authorization; independent promotional release | No new uploads before permission. Stop future use on withdrawal, assess lawful custody; public/social copies need separate removal process. |
| Appointments: IDs, times, staff, treatment, contactEmail, notes, status/cancel reasons/reminders | Booking/agenda | Personal; treatment choices may reveal sensitive information | Basic booking purpose limited to what is needed; health details require separate permission | Tenant schedule. Replace rich reminder content with minimum useful detail; avoid health in note field. |
| Treatment payments: amount, method, date, note, attribution and balances | Manual entry, payment accounting | Personal financial record, no cards stored | Business authorization and applicable accounting duties; Convex | Accountant classifies commercial records. This is not a payment gateway for the salon. |
| Polar customer/org reference, admin email, product/subscription IDs, status, period end, checkout and invoice references | Subscription checkout/webhook, billing | Needed for purchase; no card PAN/CVC stored by Cosmela | Purchase disclosures; Polar independent roles reviewed. Polar, Convex | Applicable commercial documents ten years under mapped duty; other API/debug payloads minimized. Verify Polar country/access details. |
| Analytics IDs, events, URLs, browser/device properties | PostHog when key configured | Optional personal/pseudonymous data | Prior optional authorization; PostHog US configuration | Proposed 90-day events, 12-month browser identifier; allowlisted events/properties, route templates without IDs/query values, no replay/autocapture. |
| Session cookies, theme key, consent preference | Browser persistence | Necessary/session or user-selected preference; analytics optional | Inform necessary purposes; opt-in for optional analytics | Verify cookie lifetimes; preference 12-month proposal; exact runtime inventory pending. |
| Support email and privacy/incident cases, verification evidence | ayuda@cosmela.app | Personal; unexpected health data possible | Request handling; limited statutory retention/defense where justified | Proposed five years after resolution for minimum rights-case evidence (verify); remove unrelated attachments; ordinary support 12 months after resolution proposed. Mailbox provider/region [TODO]. |
| Security logs, IP/rate-limit records, upload metadata and audit | Auth, backend, provider logs | IP personal; secrets excluded | Necessary proportionate security and applicable authority | Proposed 30-day rolling logs; document provider limits, rate-counter expiry, scoped incident holds and access. No default raw IP in long-lived consent ledger. |

No application AI feature was identified. A future AI integration requires a new assessment. No minors are permitted; existing guardian rows must be assessed and restricted rather than bulk-deleted. Operational inactivity, subscription expiry, archiving, and an explicit closure request are different events.
